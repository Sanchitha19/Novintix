# Novintix Returns & Refunds Policy
Version: 2024.1

## Return Eligibility
- Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Products must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags intact.
- Items like innerwear, cosmetics, and personalized products are not eligible for returns due to hygiene reasons.

## Refund Process
- Once the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse (usually within 48 hours), the refund will be initiated.
- Refunds for prepaid orders will be credited back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
- For Cash on Delivery (CoD) orders, refunds will be processed to the provided bank account or Novintix Wallet within 7-10 business days.

## Automated Refund Limits
- Our automated system can process refunds up to ₹5,000 instantly.
- Refunds exceeding ₹5,000 require manual verification and approval from our finance team, which may take an additional 24-48 hours.

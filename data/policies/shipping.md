# Novintix Shipping & Delivery Policy
Version: 2024.1

## Shipping Timelines
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days. Cost: Free for orders above ₹499, otherwise ₹40.
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days. Cost: ₹150 flat rate.
- Delivery hours are 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.

## Tracking Your Order
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking ID via SMS and email. You can use the "Track Order" feature in our app or website to see the real-time status.

## Delayed Deliveries
In case of delays due to weather or logistics issues, the ETA will be updated automatically. If you don't receive your order within 7 days of the promised date, you are eligible for a ₹50 shipping credit.

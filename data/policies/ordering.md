# Ordering Issues and Account Troubleshooting

If you are experiencing issues placing an order or accessing your account, please review the common causes and solutions below:

## Why Can't I Place an Order?

There are several common reasons why you might not be able to place an order:

1. **Payment issue** — your saved card may be expired or declined. Try adding a new payment method or contacting your bank.
2. **Account suspended** — your account may be temporarily suspended due to suspicious activity or multiple failed login attempts.
3. **Item out of stock** — the item in your cart may no longer be available. Check the product page for current availability.
4. **Address issue** — your delivery address may be unserviceable or incomplete. Ensure all fields are correctly filled.
5. **Cart session expired** — try clearing your cart and adding items again.
6. **Browser/app issue** — clear your browser cache or try a different browser/device.

## Account Blocked or Suspended

Accounts are typically blocked for safety reasons, such as:
- Unusual login patterns
- Security breaches
- Policy violations

If your account is blocked, you will need to verify your identity through our secure verification portal.
